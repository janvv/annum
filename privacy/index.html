Privacy Policy

Annum · Last updated 16 August 2026

Annum does not collect any data.

Annum is a macOS app that shows the events from your Mac's Calendar app in a full-year view and lets you edit them. It reads and writes those events only on your Mac, using Apple's Calendar framework, and only after you grant calendar access. Your events are never transmitted to us, never stored on any server, and never shared with anyone. Annum has no user accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

Calendar access

macOS asks for your permission before Annum can read or change your calendars. You can withdraw that permission at any time under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars. Annum will then no longer be able to display or edit your events.

Accounts synced to your calendar

If your Mac's Calendar syncs with iCloud, Google Calendar, or another account, that synchronisation is handled by macOS and by the respective provider under their own privacy policies. Annum takes no part in it and communicates with no such service directly.

Changes to this policy

If Annum's data handling ever changes, this policy will be updated and the date above revised.
